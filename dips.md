      <h2>What is DIPS?</h2>
      <p>DIPS is a social sport app for people who would rather train together than alone. Open the live map, see what's happening around you — a run in the park, a football kickabout, a yoga session — and join in with one tap. Before you go, you can see who else is coming, how the organiser is rated, and message them. Want to lead? Host your own event in under a minute and let your neighbourhood find it.</p>
      <div class="stat-strip stat-strip--spaced">

        <div class="stat"><b>2-way</b><span>Ratings after every session</span></div>
        <div class="stat"><b>0</b><span>Ad tracking</span></div>
      </div>

    <section class="feature-section reveal">
      <h2>Built on trust</h2>
      <p>Meeting strangers for a workout only works if you know who's going to turn up. That's why DIPS is designed to make good behaviour visible.</p>
      <div class="features-grid">
        <div class="feature-card">
          <div class="feature-icon">⭐</div>
          <h3>Ratings both ways</h3>
          <p>After a session, participants rate the organiser and organisers rate their group. Every rating shows how many people gave it, so a single 5-star review can't pass as fifty.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="feature-card">
          <div class="feature-icon">✅</div>
          <h3>Reliability</h3>
          <p>Organisers confirm who actually showed up. Your reliability appears on your profile, so people who sign up and don't come are easy to spot.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="feature-card">
          <div class="feature-icon">🛡️</div>
          <h3>Report &amp; block</h3>
          <p>Every profile, event and chat lets you report or block someone in one tap. Every report is reviewed.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="feature-card">
          <div class="feature-icon">🔒</div>
          <h3>Privacy first</h3>
          <p>Private profiles, no advertising ID and no tracking prompt. Analytics are off unless you opt in, and profile pictures are hidden from screenshots and screen recordings.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="feature-section reveal">
      <span class="eyebrow">For trainers, studios &amp; clubs</span>
      <h2>Bring your sessions to DIPS</h2>
      <p>Trainers, studios, clubs and schools get a business profile, with a description, address, website and Instagram, plus followers and rating counts. Run a regular course as a series, see who follows you, and message everyone signed up at once. Paid course spots are coming, with participants paying by Apple Pay or card.</p>
    </section>

    <section class="feature-section reveal">
      <h2>How DIPS works</h2>
      <p>From "let's train" to training, in minutes.</p>
      <div class="steps">
        <div class="step"><div class="num"></div><h3>Discover nearby</h3><p>Open the live map or the nearby list and filter by the sports you like.</p></div>
        <div class="step"><div class="num"></div><h3>Join or host</h3><p>Join with one tap, or get on the waiting list. Or create your own event and pick the sport, place, time and group size.</p></div>
        <div class="step"><div class="num"></div><h3>Meet &amp; move</h3><p>Chat with your group, show up and train. Afterwards, rate each other.</p></div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <section class="feature-section reveal">
      <h2>Create events from the web</h2>
      <p>Organising a regular meetup or a whole series of sessions is much easier on a keyboard. Sign in with your DIPS account in the browser and add events from your computer. They sync straight into the app, where members can find and join them.</p>
      <p class="section-action"><a href="{{ '/dips/events/' | relative_url }}" class="btn btn-primary">Go to Manage events</a></p>
    </section>

    <section class="feature-section feature-section-compact reveal">
      <h2>Built with</h2>
      <p>DIPS is a native iOS app written in <strong>Swift</strong> and <strong>SwiftUI</strong>, with <strong>MapKit</strong> for the live map. Accounts, events, chats and push notifications run on <strong>Firebase</strong> (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Functions, App Check and Cloud Messaging), and you can sign in with <strong>Apple</strong> or <strong>Google</strong>.</p>
    </section>
